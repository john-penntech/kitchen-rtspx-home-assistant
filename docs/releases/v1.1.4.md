**More reliable live camera video.** This update adds a compatible video stream when WebRTC is unavailable or your browser cannot play the camera's HEVC video. Your existing camera settings and entity IDs are preserved.

Requires **Home Assistant 2026.8.1 or later with its built-in, managed go2rtc server**. External go2rtc servers are not supported by this fallback.

### Updating

1. Create a Home Assistant backup, then select **Update** to download.
2. If updating other integrations, download those too. Restart Home Assistant **once**, when a brief interruption to cameras, automations, and voice control is convenient.
3. Open **Settings > System**, select the power menu, then **Restart Home Assistant**. A restart loads the updated integration code; reloading the integration or refreshing your browser does not replace it.
4. Reopen your camera dashboard and confirm that live video moves.

The update does not restart Home Assistant automatically.
